YAiRO **1.0** is here! This is the first full release. It brings everything
from the pre-releases since 0.0.5, and that is a lot.

## Le Mans Ultimate

YAiRO now supports **Le Mans Ultimate** next to iRacing. Choose the
simulation in the Control Center.

- **Damage overlay** with 8 damage zones, flat or lost wheels and warnings for overheating
- **Virtual Energy** for Hypercar and LMGT3
- **Live tire data** while driving
- **Track limit points** and pit stops in the standings

For LMU, turn on `Settings → Gameplay → Enable Plugins` and restart LMU
completely.

## New overlays and options

- New **delta bar** overlay
- **Country flags**, **car manufacturer logos** and **team names** in the standings
- **Predicted iRating change** for every driver
- **Last lap time** option in standings and relative
- **Real world clock** and **estimated total laps**
- Pit stop counter and penalty info
- Pit limiter and low fuel warning LEDs plus more rev LED colors
- Last lap fuel usage and estimated pit stop time in the fuel overlay
- Penalty points

## Improvements

- Overlays hide automatically when you are not in a session or the sim is not running
- New news feed in the Control Center (you are reading it right now)
- New data reader for iRacing, built into YAiRO
- And many more

Thank you to everyone who tested the pre-releases! If something doesn't work
as expected, please
