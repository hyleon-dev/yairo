YAiRO now supports **Le Mans Ultimate**! All overlays you know from iRacing
work in LMU too, plus a few that only LMU makes possible.

![Standings in Le Mans Ultimate](img/lmu-header.jpg)

## Highlights

- **Damage overlay** with 8 damage zones, flat or lost wheels and warnings for overheating
- **Virtual Energy** in the fuel overlay for Hypercar and LMGT3
- **Live tire data** while driving, not only in the pit lane
- **Track map** with the official layout and the pit lane
- **Track limit points** and pit stops in the standings
- Overlays hide automatically in the main menu

## Setup

1. Choose **Le Mans Ultimate** in the Control Center (section "Simulation").
2. In LMU, turn on `Settings → Gameplay → Enable Plugins`.
3. Restart LMU completely.

Your iRacing settings stay as they are. Every simulator keeps its own overlay
layout and options.

LMU support is brand new. If something doesn't work as expected, please
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/hyleon-dev/yairo/issues).
